About GOODFUN TEAM

GOODFUN TEAM is a Taiwanese creative collective founded in 2025, formed by l72uo_ (visual design and painting) and rotion87 (photography and conceptual practice).

Their work begins with quiet observations of everyday life — translating the visible into art, while guiding viewers back into invisible, reflective thought. The collective’s practice revolves around a continuous cycle of observation, thinking, and transformation.
