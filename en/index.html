GOODFUN TEAM

An independent art collective. We focus on structures, traces of use, and states of pause in everyday environments. Through photography, printed matter and installations, we capture the small connections forgotten in the city.

  • Year of Horse Card

    2026 | Postcard / Risograph print · hand-sculpted clay figures

    Postcard / Risograph (stencil) printing. Themed around the Year of the Horse, the character prototypes were first hand-sculpted in clay and painted, then photographed and separated into color layers for printing. In the image, GOODFUN TEAM's characters — the Mouse and the Ferret — ride racehorse-shaped rocking horses, a metaphor for how, while chasing a goal, we may seem to be moving forward yet remain in the same place.

  • IN PLACE

    2025 | 48-page photography fold-out zine × hand-cast concrete brick

    Photography fold-out zine × concrete object. The work began after relocating to Taipei in late 2024, with continued observation of the hollow bricks repeatedly used on the streets. Through photography and the fold-out zine format, we imagine each brick fulfilling its duty in its place.

  • Soft Drinks

    2025 | Photography × packaging design · scarf in a pull-tab aluminum can

    Photography × packaging design. Images of alcohol cans collected between 2023 and 2025 are transformed into silk scarf prints and sealed inside aluminum cans. The scarf can only be taken out by “opening the can”, echoing the irreversibility of drinking.

  • 30 Hours 2nd Ed.

    2025 2nd edition (1st edition 2024) | A5 photography zine · 30 pages · film

    Photography zine (A5). Built on the concept of the “30-hour clock”, it records the urban rhythms of Shinjuku and Shibuya on film through actual walking and sensing.

  • Hand-it

    2025 | Stationery design · sticky notes

    Stationery design. Inspired by the everyday experience of writing important things on your hand and then struggling to wash them off. The “palm” is turned into a sticky note you can post anywhere.

  • PastePort

    2025 | A5 sticker book · 30 pages

    Sticker book. Starting from street sticker culture, it gathers the sticker scenes that might appear on the streets of Taiwan and compiles them into a zine.

  • PASSTER

    2026 | Sticker series · extension of PASTE BOOK

    Stickers. Street objects and slogans are turned into stickers that can be posted freely. Whoever passes through the city, pauses and sticks one down becomes a “PASSTER” who leaves a trace behind.

  • Lucky Candle

    2026 | Resin, paper · contains three fortune slips

    Resin and paper. Inspired by the classic Taiwanese birthday “question-mark candle”, designed as a lucky charm. It symbolizes “the last wish, saved for yourself”.

  • PIPO-KUN Papier-mâché

    2025 | Papier-mâché (hariko) · approx. 20 cm

    Three-dimensional character in papier-mâché (hariko). The mascot of the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department, “Pipo-kun”, is recreated in papier-mâché, turning a public character into a personal collectible through handmade translation.

  • GOODFUN Radio

    2025 | Sculptural installation · approx. 25 cm

    Installation. Using sound as a metaphor for “signal” and “connection”, it portrays characters receiving and responding to one another amid the city's noise.
