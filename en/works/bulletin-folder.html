Gufang Village Bulletin Board

2026 | Clear PP two-pocket folder · 220 × 310 mm · reverse print with white ink

Stationery design. Modeled on the neighborhood bulletin boards on Taiwanese street corners, the folder carries the board’s official notices, “No advertising” and “Keeping our streets clean is everyone’s job,” while its pockets fill up with loan cards, plumber stickers and pizza flyers. Printed in reverse over a white-ink base, so the notices and the ads share the same clear layer.

Stuff it with your own flyers

Pick a few photos or take one, watch them slide into the bulletin board, then save the result as a Stories image or video.
