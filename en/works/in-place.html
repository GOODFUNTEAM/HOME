IN PLACE

2025 | 48-page photography fold-out zine × hand-cast concrete brick

Photography fold-out zine × concrete object. The work began after relocating to Taipei in late 2024, with continued observation of the hollow bricks repeatedly used on the streets. Through photography and the fold-out zine format, we imagine each brick fulfilling its duty in its place.
