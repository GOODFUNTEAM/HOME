Soft Drinks

2025 | Photography × packaging design · scarf in a pull-tab aluminum can

Photography × packaging design. Images of alcohol cans collected between 2023 and 2025 are transformed into silk scarf prints and sealed inside aluminum cans. The scarf can only be taken out by “opening the can”, echoing the irreversibility of drinking.
